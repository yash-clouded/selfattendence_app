# 📱 SelfAttendance App - User Manual

Welcome to **SelfAttendance**, your personal class manager designed to keep your attendance on track with style. Below are the instructions on how to use every feature of the app.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Onboarding
When you first launch the app, you will be greeted with an onboarding screen. 
- **Personalization**: Enter your name to personalize the app. This is compulsory as it sets up your profile.
- **Privacy**: All your data, including your name and subjects, is stored locally on your device.

---

## 📅 Managing Subjects

### 1. Adding a Subject
Tap the **"+" (Add)** button on the home screen.
- **Details**: Enter the subject name and your target attendance percentage (e.g., 75%).
- **Schedule Type**: 
    - **Weekly**: Select specific days of the week (Mon-Sun).
    - **Monthly Dates**: Select specific dates of the month (1-31).
- **Set Timings**: For each selected day, tap **"Set Time"** to specify when the class starts.
- **Reminders**: Choose how many minutes before the class you want a notification from your phone's calendar app (e.g., 10 mins before).
- **Temporary Classes**: Toggle "Is this a temporary class?" if the subject has a specific end date (e.g., a short workshop).

### 2. Editing or Deleting
- **Edit**: Tap on a subject to see details, then tap the **Pencil icon** in the top right. You can modify any detail, including timings and targets.
- **Delete**: Swipe **Left** on a subject card on the home screen to remove it completely.

---

## ✅ Tracking Attendance

### 1. Daily Marking
On the home screen, each subject card shows your current status.
- Tap **"PRESENT"** or **"ABSENT"** to log your attendance for today.
- The app automatically calculates your percentage and updates your streak.

### 2. Calendar View
Tap on a subject card to open its **Calendar View**.
- Highlighted dots indicate your class days.
- Green circles = Present.
- Red circles = Absent.
- **Holidays**: Tap on a date and select **"Mark as Holiday"** ☀️. This class will be ignored from your attendance percentage calculations.
- **Notes**: Tap a date to add specific notes (e.g., "Teacher was late" or "Important lecture").

---

## 📊 Analytics & Insights

### 1. Live Calculations
Every subject card shows:
- **Attendance Percentage**: Your real-time standing.
- **Attend/Bunk Advice**: 
    - If you are below target, it tells you exactly **how many more classes** you must attend.
    - If you are above target, it tells you **how many classes you can safety bunk** while staying above your goal.

### 2. Analysis Sheet
Swipe **Right** on a subject card or tap the **Pie Chart** icon to see a detailed breakdown of your attendance history and monthly trends.

### 3. Today's Schedule
The top of the home screen features **"Today's Schedule"**.
- It lists all your classes for today in chronological order.
- The **"NEXT"** badge automatically highlights your upcoming class based on the current time.

---

## 🔔 Notifications & Calendar Sync

The app integrates directly with your **Device's Calendar App**.
- When you add a subject with timings, the app creates a recurring event in your phone's calendar.
- **Reminders**: You will receive notifications based on the "Reminder" timing you set in the app.
- **Sync**: Ensure you give the app permission to "Write to Calendar" when prompted.

---

## 🎨 Design & Tips
- **Personal Greeting**: See "HI! [Name]" at the top left.
- **Visual Distinction**: Blue dots represent class days, Orange icons represent holidays.
- **Modern UI**: The app uses a premium Dark Mode inspired by iOS aesthetics.
